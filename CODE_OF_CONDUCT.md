# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Backend Toolkit - BaaS project a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative experience for everyone.

We expect all contributors and community members to interact professionally and respectfully.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate towards others.
- Communicate constructively.
- Welcome different ideas and approaches.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Focus on improving the project and helping others learn.
- Respect the time and effort of other contributors.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Offensive or inappropriate comments.
- Sharing private or personal information without permission.
- Intentionally disrupting the project or its contributors.
- Any behaviour that makes the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Contribution Discussions

Technical disagreements are normal in an open-source project.

When disagreements occur, contributors should focus on:

1. The project's goals.
2. Technical reasoning.
3. Evidence and documentation.
4. Constructive discussion.

Personal attacks or disrespectful communication are not acceptable.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or observe behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainers through the official communication channel of the project.

Reports will be handled respectfully and as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated, including removing inappropriate content, restricting participation, or taking other measures necessary to maintain a healthy project environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, discussions, issues, pull requests, and other interactions related to the Backend Toolkit - BaaS community.

## Acknowledgement

By participating in this project, contributors agree to follow these guidelines and help maintain a respectful and collaborative open-source community.
